Add the methods orchestrator: `MethodsOrchestrator` implements `IMethodsOrchestrator` from the method registry, the action codec registry and the list of device bindings the runtime meets (required; `none` is always met), checks all three whole at construction, holds no provider and builds every ctx from the request alone. `signingInput` and `enrollInput` throw `KitRefusalError` for a method no implementation serves or a binding the runtime cannot meet; `replyFrom` and `configFrom` answer `method-unsupported`, `request-unsupported` or `device-unavailable` as typed failures, and pass on a method's own failure as a fresh frozen record; `verify` throws for a place other than the request's own or an unserved method, and the `IMethodsOrchestrator.verify` comment now says so. `describeRequest` now hands an implementation's `describe` a checked, frozen copy of the request rather than the caller's object. New constants: `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_UNBOUND`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_METHOD_UNSUPPORTED`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_REQUEST_UNSUPPORTED`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_DEVICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_METHOD_MEMBERS`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_METHOD_CODEC_MEMBERS`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_ACTION_CODEC_MEMBERS`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_REGISTRY_MEMBERS`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_UNSERVED_METHOD_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_UNMET_BINDING_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_PLACE_MISMATCH_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_REPEAT_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_ZERO_KEY_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_FOREIGN_ACTION_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_UNKNOWN_VALUE_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_VERDICT_ANSWER_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_BYTES_ANSWER_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_INPUT_ANSWER_MESSAGE`, `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_ENROLL_ANSWER_MESSAGE` and `METHODS_ORCHESTRATOR_ENROLL_KINDS`.
